Direction 01

Light Community Hub

A bright, accessible homepage direction focused on events, membership value, and frequent updates.

Best fitMain BSNL homepage and regular event communications.
StrengthClear, approachable, easier for visitors to scan quickly.
RiskNeeds strong images for highlights to avoid feeling too generic.

Direction 02

Premium Scientific Forum

A darker, glossier institutional direction with stronger presence for flagship moments.

Best fitFlagship event pages, sponsor-facing pages, and a more formal homepage.
StrengthFeels more distinctive, premium, and partner-ready.
RiskCould feel heavier if the homepage needs to be updated often with everyday events.

Reusable content

  • Events: date, location, registration link, status.
  • News/Highlights: recap, images, category, related event.
  • Team and alumni: role, group, contact/social link.
  • Partnership: sponsor profile, visit proposal, event connection.
Maintenance model

How updates should work after launch

The design should not require committee members to edit page layouts for routine updates.

Events

Create a new event entry. Homepage automatically updates next session and calendar preview.

Highlights

Publish an event recap or activity record. The highlights carousel updates from recent posts.

Team

Update committee members from a structured team list rather than manually editing cards.

Assets

Keep a shared image bank for event photography, partner logos, and recurring BSNL graphics.
